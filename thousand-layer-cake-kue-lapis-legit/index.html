Thousand Layer Cake Kue Lapis Legit

Thousand-layer cake (kue lapis legit), from Coconut & Sambal by Lara Lee. Serves 24. A spiced, buttery Indonesian sponge built from thin golden layers grilled one at a time — cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger and cardamom, 22 egg yolks, and enough butter that it keeps at least a week in the fridge. Also known as spekkoek ('bacon cake') in Dutch, for its streaky appearance.

Ingredients

  • 370 grams unsalted butter, softened, plus extra for greasing
  • 150 grams condensed milk
  • 22 units medium egg yolks, at room temperature
  • 150 grams icing (powdered) sugar
  • 70 grams plain (all-purpose) flour
  • 25 grams milk powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1/4 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/4 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt

You will need: a 20 cm (8 in) round cake tin and an oven with a grill (broiler) function.

Notes for even layers

  • Use only the grill (broiler) function, never the fan.
  • As the layers get higher you may need to shorten the grilling time or lower the shelf.
  • A layer is done when it turns from pale to golden — watch it constantly, it burns easily.
  • Holes form in the layers if the heat is too high; adjust the temperature as needed.
  • Each layer should take 1 1/2 to 4 minutes, depending on the oven.
  • Measure each layer for consistency: 5 tablespoons (~75 g) per layer in a 20 cm tin, spread about 3 mm thick. That's roughly 14 to 16 layers from this batter.

Directions

  1. 10 minutesPreheat the oven to 200°C / 180°C fan / gas 6. Grease a 20 cm round cake tin and line the base and sides with baking parchment.
  2. 5 minutesUsing an electric whisk, beat the 370 g softened butter with the 150 g condensed milk until pale and fluffy, about 5 minutes.
  3. 8 minutesIn a separate bowl with clean beaters, whisk the 22 egg yolks and 150 g icing sugar together until fluffy and doubled in volume.
  4. 7 minutesOn low speed, add the egg yolk mixture to the whipped butter about 2 tablespoons at a time, beating between additions and being careful not to overmix.
  5. 5 minutesSift the 70 g flour, 25 g milk powder, 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon, 1/4 tsp nutmeg, 1/4 tsp ginger, 1/4 tsp cardamom and 1/4 tsp sea salt into the bowl. Fold gently with a spatula until fully incorporated into a smooth, streak-free batter.
  6. 5 minutesSwitch the oven to the grill (broiler) function with the fan off. Spread a thin 3 mm layer of batter over the base of the tin. Bang the tin on the work surface to knock out air bubbles. Grill on the top shelf until golden, 1 1/2 to 4 minutes.
  7. 5 minutesRemove from the grill, pop any air bubbles with a cocktail stick, and flatten the layer with a fish slice or palette knife. Spread another 5 tablespoons (~75 g) of batter on top, bang the tin again, and grill another 1 1/2 to 4 minutes until golden.
  8. 60 minutesRepeat step 7 until all the batter is used, keeping the layers even.
  9. 90 minutesCool in the tin for 30 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack to cool completely. Serve in small slices — it is very rich.

Full example schedule

  1. 1:00 PM: Preheat, grease and line the tin
  2. 1:10 PM: Beat butter and condensed milk
  3. 1:15 PM: Whisk egg yolks and icing sugar
  4. 1:25 PM: Combine, then fold in the dry ingredients
  5. 1:35 PM: Switch to grill; first layer
  6. 1:40 PM–2:45 PM: Remaining layers, ~4 minutes each
  7. 2:45 PM: Cool 30 minutes in the tin, then on a rack
  8. 4:15 PM: Slice and serve
