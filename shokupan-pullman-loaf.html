Yeasted shokupan pullman loaf (2 loaves)

Ingredients

Tangzhong

  • 50 g bread flour
  • 250 g whole milk

Main dough

  • 700 grams bread flour
  • 110 grams granulated sugar
  • 4 teaspoon (16 g) diamond kosher crystal salt
  • 2 packets (12 g) active dry yeast
  • 16 g milk powder
  • 2 large egg
  • 240 g milk
  • 90 g (6 tablespoons) unsalted butter, cut into pieces
  • ~300 g tangzhong

Egg Wash

  • 1 egg
  • 1 tablespoon water
  • Pinch of salt

Directions

Example schedule

  • 9 AM: Make the tangzhong
  • 10 AM: Mix dough
  • 11:45 AM: Shape dough
  • 12:15 PM: Bake
  • 12:45 PM: Cool
  • 1:45 PM: Eat

Make the tangzhong (9 AM)

Whisk 50 g flour and 250 g whole milk together until it achieves a smooth consistency. Pour into a non-stick pan and heat over medium heat, stirring constantly until it achieves the consistency of thin mashed potatoes. A spatula should leave a trail. If you have a thermometer, it will be about 155 F degrees. Let cool for 30 to 45 minutes in the refrigerator until the mixture is room temperature.

Mix (10 AM)

Whisk the dry ingredients (700 g bread flour, 110 g granulated sugar, 4 teaspoon (12 g) DCK salt, 2 packet (12 g) active dry yeast, 16 g milk powder) in the bowl of a stand mixer. Add 2 eggs, 240 g milk, and the tangzhong, with paddle attachment, until completely combined. Let rest for 10 minutes. Switch to dough hook attachment. Add the butter pieces, one every few seconds, and mix on medium speed for 15 to 20 minutes. Dough should be very stretchy. Let rise for about 1 hour in an oiled bowl until doubled in size. This should take about an hour at 75F°.

Shape (11:45 PM)

Shape into six balls. Arrange three each in 2 oiled 9 inch by 5 inch bread pans, cover with plastic wrap, and allow to rise about 40 minutes, until it is about 3/4 of the height of the bread pan.

Preheat oven to 350°F.

Bake (12:15 PM)

Brush the loaf with the egg wash (if not using pullman lid) and bake for 30 to 35 minutes at 350°F. Remove the loaf from the pan and cool on a wire rack. Let it cool completely.

  • 9 AM: Make the tangzhong
  • 10 AM: Mix dough (+30 minutes to 1 hour)
  • 10:15 AM: Add butter and mix (+15 minutes)
  • 10:35 AM: Let rise for an hour (+20 minutes)
  • 11:45 AM: Shape dough (+70 minutes)
  • 12:15 PM: Proof for 30 minutes
  • 12:45 PM: Bake
  • 1:15 PM: Cool
  • 1:45 PM: Eat
