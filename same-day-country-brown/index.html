Same Day Country Brown

Bread

A same-day 30% whole wheat country loaf. It keeps the same flour blend (70% white, 30% whole wheat), the same 78% hydration and 2.2% salt, but the levain is replaced with instant yeast for lift plus sourdough discard for flavor. The yeast amount and timings follow a standard same-day yeasted loaf. You skip the 7–9 hour levain build, the 12–15 hour bulk and the 4 hour proof: about 8 hours from start to finish instead of about 27.

NOTE: These amounts assume the discard is 100% hydration (equal weights of flour and water). If your starter is kept at a different hydration, keep the total water at 780g: the final-dough water is 780g minus the water in the discard. The 2 tbsp of flour in the discard is assumed to be white.

Ingredients

  • 600 grams white flour (4 2/3 cups)
  • 300 grams whole wheat flour (2 1/3 cups)
  • 680 grams water, 90–95°F / 32–35°C (scant 3 cups)
  • 22 grams fine sea salt (1 tbsp + 1 tsp)
  • 3 grams instant yeast (3/4 tsp)
  • 200 grams sourdough discard, at room temperature (3/4 cup)

Baker's percentages (totals, discard included): 1000g flour = 100%; 780g water = 78%; 22g salt = 2.2%; 3g yeast = 0.3%; 100g flour from discard = 10% pre-fermented (the original uses 12% levain).

Directions

  1. Take the 200g discard out of the fridge so it comes up to room temperature. Cold discard will pull the dough below its target temperature. If it is still cold at mix time, use water at the warm end of the range, or up to 100°F.
  2. 30 minutesAutolyse: Mix 600g white flour, 300g whole wheat flour and 680g water at 90–95°F by hand in a 12-quart tub just until incorporated. Cover and rest 20–30 minutes.
  3. 10 minutesMix: Sprinkle 22g salt and 3g yeast evenly over the dough, then add 200g discard. Mix by hand with a wet hand, using the pincer method and folding until fully integrated. Target dough temperature is 77–78°F / 25–26°C.
  4. 5 hoursBulk ferment: Cover. Give the dough 3–4 folds, all within the first 2 hours. Bulk is done when the dough is about 2½ times its original volume, about 5 hours after mixing. Whole wheat and discard can speed things up, so start checking at 4 hours.
  5. 10 minutesDivide and shape: Ease the dough onto a lightly floured surface, cut it into 2 equal pieces, shape each into a medium-tight ball and place seam side down in floured proofing baskets.
  6. Preheat: Put 2 Dutch ovens with their lids on a middle rack and preheat to 475°F / 245°C for at least 45 minutes. (3:05 PM)
  7. 75 minutesProof: Cover the baskets and proof about 1¼ hours at about 70°F, or about 1 hour in a warmer kitchen. Use the finger-dent test and start checking at 1 hour. Yeasted loaves can go from perfectly proofed to slightly collapsed in about 15 minutes.
  8. 55 minutesBake: Turn each loaf out seam side up into a hot Dutch oven. Bake covered 30 minutes, then uncover and bake 20–25 minutes more, until dark chestnut brown all over. Check at 15 minutes uncovered. With one Dutch oven, refrigerate the second loaf 20 minutes before the first one goes in, and reheat the pot 5 minutes between loaves.
  9. 20 minutesCool on a rack for at least 20 minutes before slicing; an hour is better.

Variation: More flavor, bake next morning

Mix at 1 PM, shape at about 6 PM, then proof the loaves in the fridge, covered, for 12–14 hours. Bake straight from the fridge at about 8 AM. There is still no levain feed, but the long cold proof adds tang and blistering on the crust.

Tuning

  • Faster: Use 4g yeast (1 tsp). This cuts roughly 30–45 minutes from bulk.
  • Cool kitchen (below about 70°F): Use 4g yeast and expect bulk to run toward 6 hours.
  • More tang: Use up to 300g discard and cut the final water and flour to keep the totals at 1000g flour and 780g water. For example, with 300g discard at 100% hydration use 630g water and 550g white flour. More than that and the acid starts to weaken the gluten.

Full example schedule

  1. 8:30 AM: Take discard out of fridge
  2. 9:00 AM: Autolyse
  3. 9:30 AM: Mix in salt, yeast, discard
  4. 9:40 AM: Bulk ferment (folds through 11:30 AM)
  5. 2:40 PM: Divide and shape
  6. 2:50 PM: Proof
  7. 3:05 PM: Preheat oven with Dutch ovens
  8. 4:05 PM: Bake (covered 30 min, uncovered 20–25 min)
  9. 5:00 PM: Cool
  10. 5:20 PM: Slice
