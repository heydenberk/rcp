French Toast

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • 30 grams milk (2 tbsp)
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 slices bread (brioche or challah works best)

Directions

  1. 1 minuteCrack egg into a shallow bowl or dish. Add milk, vanilla, and cinnamon. Whisk until well combined.
  2. 2 minutesHeat butter in a skillet over medium heat until melted and foamy.
  3. 1 minuteDip each slice of bread into the egg mixture, letting it soak for 10-15 seconds per side.
  4. 5 minutesPlace soaked bread in the hot skillet. Cook until golden brown, 2-3 minutes per side.
  5. Serve immediately with maple syrup, powdered sugar, or fresh fruit.
