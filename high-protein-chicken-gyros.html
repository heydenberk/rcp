High-Protein Chicken Gyros with Yogurt Flatbread

Lean grilled chicken in a yogurt-lemon marinade, soft nonfat-yogurt flatbreads, and a lightened high-protein tzatziki. Nonfat Greek yogurt does triple duty across all three components to keep fat low while protein stays high.

Makes: 4 gyrosEstimated Total Time: ~1 hour (plus 30 min – 12 hr marinade)Approx. per gyro: 576 cal · 59g protein · 49g carbs · 14g fat

Chicken

Ingredient Amount
Boneless skinless chicken breast 1.5 lb
Plain nonfat Greek yogurt ½ cup
Extra-virgin olive oil 1 tbsp
Lemon, juiced 1
Garlic cloves, minced 3
Dried oregano 1 tbsp
Paprika 1 tsp
Ground cumin 1 tsp
Ground coriander 1 tsp
Salt 1 tsp

Yogurt Flatbread

Ingredient Amount
All-purpose flour 1.5 cups
Plain nonfat Greek yogurt 1 cup
Baking powder 1 tsp
Salt ½ tsp
Extra-virgin olive oil 1 tbsp (for dough and pan)

Lightened Tzatziki

Ingredient Amount
Plain nonfat Greek yogurt 1 cup
Extra-virgin olive oil 1 tbsp
English cucumber, grated and squeezed dry ½
Garlic cloves, minced 2
Lemon, juiced ½
Fresh dill, chopped 2 tbsp
Salt pinch

Veggies

Ingredient Amount
Tomatoes, diced 2
Red onion, thinly sliced ½
Romaine lettuce, shredded 2 cups

Method

Marinate the Chicken

Whisk the ½ cup yogurt, 1 tbsp olive oil, juice of 1 lemon, 3 cloves garlic, 1 tbsp oregano, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp coriander, and 1 tsp salt. Add the chicken (butterflied or pounded to even thickness) and coat well. Marinate refrigerated at least 30 minutes (up to 12 hours; not longer, or the yogurt over-tenderizes the meat).

Make the Tzatziki

Stir together the 1 cup yogurt, 1 tbsp olive oil, grated cucumber, 2 cloves garlic, juice of ½ lemon, 2 tbsp dill, and a pinch of salt. Refrigerate so the flavors meld while you cook.

Mix the Flatbread Dough

Combine the 1.5 cups flour, 1 tsp baking powder, and ½ tsp salt, then stir in the 1 cup yogurt and about half the olive oil until a shaggy dough forms. Knead on a floured surface 2-3 minutes until smooth. Divide into 4 balls and roll each thin.

Cook the Flatbreads

Heat a dry or lightly oiled skillet over medium-high. Cook each flatbread until puffed and charred in spots, about 1.5-2 minutes per side. Keep warm under a towel.

Grill the Chicken

Wipe off excess marinade. Grill or sauté over medium-high heat 4-6 minutes per side until internal temp hits 165°F. Rest 5 minutes, then slice thin.

Assemble

Lay sliced chicken on each warm flatbread. Top with tomatoes, red onion, romaine, and a generous spoon of tzatziki. Fold and eat.

Notes & Tweaks

  • Yogurt choice matters most. Use a 0% Greek yogurt with ~23-25g protein per cup (Fage Total 0%, Chobani Non-Fat). "Greek-style" yogurts are often lower in protein — check the label if tracking tightly.
  • More protein, fewer carbs: replace 2-3 tbsp of the flour with vital wheat gluten.
  • The olive oil is intentional. Kept to 1 tbsp per component — it carries flavor and helps absorb the veggies' fat-soluble nutrients without spiking total fat.
  • Marinade macros vary with how much clings vs. drips off during cooking; scraping the chicken clean drops the fat slightly.
