Philly Fish House Punch

The 1732 Schuylkill Fishing Company original. Real oleo-saccharum, real peach brandy (schnapps doesn't count), cold black tea. Makes about 5.9 liters — fifty small cups. There's no ice in the bowl, so the whole thing needs to get cold in the fridge. Start the oleo at least 3 hours ahead. You'll need a 2-gallon punch bowl or dispenser, or just split it between two big pitchers.

Ingredients

Oleo-saccharum

  • 16 lemons, peels only (Y-peeler, save the lemons for juicing)
  • 680 grams demerara or raw sugar (3 1/2 cups)

Punch

  • 950 ml fresh lemon juice (4 cups — the peeled lemons plus ~6 more)
  • 1500 ml Jamaican rum (two bottles, something with funk — Smith & Cross, Appleton, whatever)
  • 750 ml cognac (a bottle, VS is fine)
  • 300 ml real peach brandy (most of a 375ml bottle — aged peach eau-de-vie, not schnapps, not crème de pêche)
  • 2 liters black tea, chilled (8 cups water + 8 bags or 16 grams loose tea)
  • 1 whole nutmeg for grating, if you want
  • 3 lemons, sliced into thin wheels

Directions

  1. 15 minutesthen 3+ hoursPeel the 16 lemons in wide strips, taking as little pith as you can manage. Muddle the peels with the 680g sugar in a big bowl until the sugar's wet and smells like lemon candy. Cover it and walk away for at least 3 hours (overnight's fine), giving it another smash whenever you walk past. This is the oleo-saccharum and it's the whole point of the recipe.
  2. 10 minutesthen chillMake the tea: 8 bags or 16g black tea in 8 cups just-boiled water for 4 minutes — no longer or it goes harsh. Cool it down, then into the fridge.
  3. 20 minutesJuice the peeled lemons plus a few extras until you've got 950 ml. Strain it, fridge it.
  4. 5 minutesPour the 950 ml lemon juice over the oleo and stir until all the sugar's dissolved. Fish out the peels and toss them.
  5. 5 minutesInto the punch bowl: the lemon-oleo mix, 1500 ml rum, 750 ml cognac, 300 ml peach brandy. Stir, add the 2 liters tea. Taste — it should be strong but not mean. Splash in more tea if it bites.
  6. 0 minutesthen 2+ hoursFridge the whole thing until party time. Cold is everything here since there's no ice. It also just gets better as it sits — making it a day ahead is a good move.
  7. 5 minutesFloat the lemon wheels, grate a little nutmeg over top if you're feeling it. Pour small — 3–4 oz cups. Each little cup is basically a full cocktail and this stuff sneaks up on people.

Can't find real peach brandy? 150 ml peach liqueur + 150 ml extra cognac is the least-bad fallback, but try to get the real thing (Kuchan and Peach Street both make one). Plain water instead of tea is what they did in 1732; the tea's better. Want it lighter? Add 1–1.5 liters cold water with the tea — takes it from ~18% down to wine strength, which is roughly what the ice melt would've done back when this went over a big block.

Full example schedule

  1. 12:00 PM: Peel lemons, muddle with sugar, rest 3+ hours (oleo-saccharum)
  2. 12:20 PM: Make tea, cool, fridge
  3. 2:00 PM: Juice lemons, strain, fridge
  4. 3:00 PM: Dissolve the oleo in the lemon juice, strain out peels
  5. 3:05 PM: Add rum, cognac, peach brandy, tea; taste
  6. 3:15 PM: Fridge until serving
  7. 5:30 PM: Lemon wheels, nutmeg, small cups
