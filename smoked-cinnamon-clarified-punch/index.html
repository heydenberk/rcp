Smoked Cinnamon Clarified Punch

Drink

A tequila and mezcal milk punch with lime, apple cider, and a smoked cinnamon syrup. Pouring the cocktail into whole milk curdles the milk, and straining it through a coffee filter leaves a clear, silky punch that keeps in the fridge for weeks. Serves 10–12. Start the day before: the syrup has to cool, the punch rests 3 hours, and straining takes another 6–8 hours.

NOTE: Gram weights are converted from the original cup measures using each liquid's density (spirits assume about 40% ABV), so either measure works.

Ingredients

Smoked cinnamon syrup

  • 400 grams sugar (2 cups)
  • 475 grams water (2 cups)
  • 8 cinnamon sticks (4 per cup of sugar)

Punch

  • 670 grams tequila (3 cups; 710 ml)
  • 670 grams mezcal (3 cups; 710 ml)
  • 610 grams freshly squeezed lime juice (2½ cups; about 20–25 limes)
  • 620 grams apple cider (2½ cups)
  • 715 grams smoked cinnamon syrup (2½ cups)
  • 305 grams whole milk (1¼ cups)

To serve

  • Large ice cubes
  • Cinnamon sticks, for smoking the glass and garnish

Directions

  1. 45 minutesSyrup: Put the 400g sugar and 475g water in a pot. Carefully light the 8 cinnamon sticks on fire, let them burn for about 10 seconds, and add them to the pot. Simmer over low heat, stirring occasionally, until the sugar has dissolved, about 10–15 minutes. Remove from the heat and cool completely; an ice bath speeds this up. Once cool, remove the cinnamon sticks. This makes about 870g (3 cups), a little more than the punch needs.
  2. 5 minutesMix: Combine the 670g tequila, 670g mezcal, 610g lime juice, 620g apple cider, and 715g syrup in a large bowl or pitcher and stir.
  3. 3 hoursCurdle: Put the 305g whole milk in a separate large bowl. Pour the cocktail into the milk, not the milk into the cocktail, and stir well. Cover and let sit at room temperature for at least 3 hours.
  4. 6–8 hoursStrain: Set a coffee filter in a fine-mesh strainer over a bowl; use several strainers for this volume. Pour the mixture into the strainer. Don't stir or press on the curds: they form the filter that clarifies the punch. If the first cup through is cloudy, pour it back through. Straining takes about 6–8 hours.
  5. 5 minutes~10 PM Day 1Store: Discard the filters and curds and store the clarified punch in sealed jars or bottles in the fridge for up to 4 weeks.
  6. Serve: Pour over a large ice cube. Optionally smoke the glass with a lit cinnamon stick first, and garnish with a smoked cinnamon stick. (Day 2)

Full example schedule

  1. 10:30 AM Day 1: Make the smoked cinnamon syrup; cool in an ice bath
  2. 11:40 AM Day 1: Juice the limes; remove the cinnamon sticks from the syrup
  3. 12:00 PM Day 1: Combine the punch ingredients
  4. 12:05 PM Day 1: Pour into the milk, stir, cover, rest 3 hours
  5. 3:05 PM Day 1: Strain through coffee filters (6–8 hours)
  6. ~10 PM Day 1: Bottle and refrigerate
  7. Day 2: Serve over ice with a smoked cinnamon stick
