Overnight Refrigerator Focaccia

Ingredients

  • 512 grams all-purpose or bread flour (4 cups)
  • 10-15 grams kosher salt (2-3 tsp)
  • 8 grams instant yeast (2 tsp)
  • 455 grams lukewarm water (2 cups)
  • 4 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 tbsp butter (for greasing pans)
  • 1-2 tsp flaky sea salt (such as Maldon)
  • 1-2 tsp fresh rosemary leaves (optional)

Directions

  1. 5 minutesWhisk together 512g flour, 10-15g kosher salt, and 8g instant yeast in a large bowl. Add 455g lukewarm water and stir with a spatula until a sticky dough forms.
  2. 30 minutesCover and let rest 30 minutes.
  3. 5 minutesWith a wet hand, perform 8-10 stretch-and-fold motions until dough is smooth.
  4. 12 hours minimumCoat dough with a drizzle of olive oil, cover tightly with a lid or plastic wrap, and refrigerate at least 12 hours (up to 72 hours).
  5. 10 minutesGrease two 8-9 inch pie plates or one 9x13-inch pan with butter. Add 1 tbsp olive oil per pie plate (or 2 tbsp for 9x13 pan). Using two forks, gently deflate dough and divide between pans (or place all in 9x13). Roll dough in oil to coat.
  6. 3-4 hoursLet rise at room temperature, uncovered, 3-4 hours, until dough is puffy and nearly filling the pan.
  7. 20 minutesPreheat oven to 425°F/218°C during final 20 minutes of rise.
  8. 5 minutesSprinkle 1-2 tsp rosemary over dough if using. Drizzle with remaining 1-2 tbsp olive oil and dimple deeply with all fingers. Sprinkle generously with flaky sea salt.
  9. 30 minutesBake 25-30 minutes until golden underneath and around the edges.
  10. 10 minutesTransfer to cooling racks and cool 10 minutes before serving.
