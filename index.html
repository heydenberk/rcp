    Cinnamon Rolls

    Twelve tangzhong-enriched rolls in a 9x13 pan under cream cheese frosting. The dough is cut into strips and rolled up individually rather than sliced off a single log, which keeps the spiral even end to end, and they bake low — 325°F to an internal 185°F — so the centers stay gooey. About 4 hours 10 minutes start to finish, most of it rising.

    Fresh Egg Pasta

    Serious Eats' workhorse egg dough — two whole eggs plus four extra yolks per 9 ounces of all-purpose flour, which lands between an all-yolk dough (rich but too stiff to roll thin) and a wetter one that sticks to the roller. Springy enough for fettuccine, pliable enough for ravioli. Makes about 1 pound.

    Fridge Proofed Country Brown

    Two crusty 30% whole wheat country loaves, risen with a little instant yeast and flavored with sourdough discard instead of a levain build. Mix in the late afternoon, shape before bed, and let the loaves proof in the fridge overnight. In the morning you only preheat and bake, straight from the fridge, with bread out around 8 AM. The cold night adds tang and a blistered crust.

    Kaffir Lime Pandan Sour

    Kaffir lime and pandan sour, batched for a party. Super juice stands in for fresh lime 1:1, kaffir leaf is infused into the rum, and the whole thing is stripped down to two jugs and no ice bath — a frozen bottle of rum chills the batch harder than a sink full of ice would. Built around one 750 ml bottle of Smith & Cross. Makes 16 drinks (eight rounds of two) plus the test drink. About 20 minutes of actual work.

    Overnight Refrigerator Focaccia

    A very wet dough — 89% hydration — that does all its work in the fridge. Five minutes of mixing the night before, one round of stretch-and-folds, then 12 hours to 3 days cold. Makes two 8–9 inch pan focaccias or one 9x13. Budget 3 to 4 hours at room temperature the next day before it goes in the oven.

    Philly Fish House Punch

    The 1732 Schuylkill Fishing Company original. Real oleo-saccharum, real peach brandy (schnapps doesn't count), cold black tea. Makes about 5.9 liters — fifty small cups. There's no ice in the bowl, so the whole thing needs to get cold in the fridge. Start the oleo at least 3 hours ahead. You'll need a 2-gallon punch bowl or dispenser, or just split it between two big pitchers.

    Thousand-layer cake (kue lapis legit), from Coconut & Sambal by Lara Lee. Serves 24. A spiced, buttery Indonesian sponge built from thin golden layers broiled one at a time — cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger and cardamom, 22 egg yolks, and enough butter that it keeps at least a week in the fridge.
