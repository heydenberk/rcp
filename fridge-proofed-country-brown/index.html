Fridge Proofed Country Brown

Bread

Two crusty 30% whole wheat country loaves, risen with a little instant yeast and flavored with sourdough discard instead of a levain build. Mix in the late afternoon, shape before bed, and let the loaves proof in the fridge overnight. In the morning you only preheat and bake, straight from the fridge, with bread out around 8 AM. The cold night adds tang and a blistered crust.

Ingredients

  • 600 grams white flour (4 2/3 cups)
  • 300 grams whole wheat flour (2 1/3 cups)
  • 680 grams water, 90–95°F / 32–35°C (scant 3 cups)
  • 22 grams fine sea salt (1 tbsp + 1 tsp)
  • 3 grams instant yeast (3/4 tsp)
  • 200 grams sourdough discard, at room temperature (3/4 cup)

Totals: 1000g flour, 780g water (78% hydration), 2.2% salt, 0.3% yeast. These assume 100% hydration discard (equal weights flour and water). If your starter is kept at a different hydration, set the water to 780g minus the water in the discard.

Directions

  1. 30 minutesTake the 200g discard out of the fridge so it comes up to room temperature. If it's still cold at mix time, use water at the warm end of the range, or up to 100°F.
  2. 30 minutesAutolyse: Mix 600g white flour, 300g whole wheat flour and 680g water at 90–95°F by hand in a 12-quart tub just until incorporated. Cover and rest.
  3. 10 minutesMix: Sprinkle 22g salt and 3g yeast evenly over the dough, then add 200g discard. Mix by hand with a wet hand, alternating pincer cuts and folds until fully integrated. Target dough temperature is 77–78°F / 25–26°C.
  4. 5 hoursBulk ferment: Cover. Give the dough 3–4 folds, all within the first 2 hours. Bulk is done when the dough is about 2½ times its original volume, about 5 hours after mixing. Start checking at 4 hours.
  5. 10 minutesDivide and shape: Ease the dough onto a lightly floured surface, cut it into 2 equal pieces, shape each into a medium-tight ball and place seam side down in floured proofing baskets.
  6. 9 hoursProof cold: Put each basket in a nonperforated plastic bag and refrigerate overnight, about 9 hours. Up to 12 hours is fine if you want to bake later.
  7. 45 minutesPreheat: Put 2 Dutch ovens with their lids on a middle rack and preheat to 475°F / 245°C for 45 minutes. Leave the loaves in the fridge.
  8. 55 minutesBake straight from the fridge: Turn each loaf out seam side up into a hot Dutch oven. Bake covered 30 minutes, then uncover and bake 20–25 minutes more, until dark chestnut brown all over. Check at 15 minutes uncovered. With one Dutch oven, leave the second loaf in the fridge and reheat the pot 5 minutes between loaves.
  9. 20 minutesCool on a rack for at least 20 minutes before slicing; an hour is better.

Tuning

  • Cool kitchen (below about 70°F): Use 4g yeast (1 tsp) so bulk still finishes by bedtime.
  • Running late: If bulk is done before you're ready for bed, shape and refrigerate early. The loaves will just have a longer cold proof.
  • More tang: Use up to 300g discard, with 630g water and 550g white flour so the totals stay at 1000g flour and 780g water.

Full example schedule

  1. 4:00 PM: Take discard out of fridge
  2. 4:30 PM: Autolyse
  3. 5:00 PM: Mix in salt, yeast, discard
  4. 5:10 PM: Bulk ferment (folds through 7:10 PM)
  5. 10:10 PM: Divide and shape
  6. 10:20 PM: Refrigerate overnight
  7. 6:15 AM Day 2: Preheat oven with Dutch ovens
  8. 7:00 AM Day 2: Bake from the fridge
  9. 7:55 AM Day 2: Cool, then slice
