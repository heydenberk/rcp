Sweet Potato Casserole

Side

Classic sweet potato casserole with a marshmallow top. Roasting the sweet potatoes whole instead of boiling them keeps the filling sweet and dense rather than watery. The base can be made a day ahead and refrigerated; the marshmallows go on at the end so they puff and brown instead of melting flat. Serves 10–12 in a 9x13 dish.

Ingredients

Base

  • 1.8 kilograms sweet potatoes (4 lb; 5–6 medium)
  • 85 grams unsalted butter (6 tbsp), melted, plus more for the dish
  • 100 grams light brown sugar (½ cup packed)
  • 120 grams heavy cream (½ cup)
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  • 6 grams Diamond Crystal kosher salt (2 tsp)

Topping

  • 280 grams mini marshmallows (one 10 oz bag)

Directions

  1. 75 minutesRoast: Heat the oven to 400°F. Prick the sweet potatoes a few times with a fork, set them on a foil-lined sheet pan, and roast until completely soft when squeezed, 60–75 minutes.
  2. 30 minutesCool and peel: Let them cool until you can handle them, about 20–30 minutes, then split them and scoop the flesh into a large bowl. Discard the skins.
  3. 10 minutesMix: Mash the sweet potatoes until smooth with a potato masher, or use a hand mixer for a fluffier texture. Beat in 85g melted butter, 100g brown sugar, 120g heavy cream, 2 eggs, 1 tsp vanilla, ½ tsp cinnamon, ¼ tsp nutmeg, and 6g salt until smooth. Taste and adjust the salt and sugar.
  4. 5 minutesAssemble: Butter a 9x13 baking dish and spread the mixture in an even layer. If making ahead, cover tightly and refrigerate for up to 2 days.
  5. 40 minutesBake the base: If refrigerated, let the dish sit out 30 minutes first. Bake covered with foil at 350°F for 25 minutes, then uncover and bake 10–15 minutes more, until hot all the way through (165°F in the center). Straight from mixing, it only needs about 25 minutes uncovered.
  6. 10 minutesMarshmallows: Scatter the 280g mini marshmallows evenly over the top. Bake 5–10 minutes, until puffed and golden, or broil 1–2 minutes, watching constantly because they go from golden to burnt quickly.
  7. 10 minutesLet it sit 10 minutes before serving.

Variations

  • Homemade marshmallows: Make the plain version of toasted-bincho-dust-marshmallows.md (leave out the charcoal) the day before, then cut into 1-inch cubes and toss them in the cornstarch and powdered sugar coating. They brown more slowly than store-bought mini marshmallows, so broil for the full 2 minutes.
  • Pecan streusel: In place of the marshmallows, rub together 100g brown sugar, 40g flour, 55g cold butter, and 100g chopped pecans, and scatter over the base before it goes in. Bake uncovered for 35–40 minutes at 350°F.
