Pumpkin Pudding Pie with Orange Zest and Cardamom Whipped Cream

Dessert

A pumpkin pie with a loose, pourable batter that rises and sets in the oven into a soft, pudding-like filling. Finished with powdered sugar and orange zest and served with cardamom whipped cream. Makes about 3 pies.

Ingredients

Pie

  • Pie crusts for 3 pies (scratch, store-bought dough, or graham cracker)
  • 2½ cups sugar
  • 1 15 oz can pumpkin puree
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp vanilla
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2½ cups milk
  • 4 tbsp butter, melted

Topping

  • 1 orange
  • 1 tsp powdered sugar

Cardamom whipped cream

  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • Pinch of nutmeg

Directions

Pie

  1. Preheat the oven to 325°F.
  2. Prepare the pie crusts.
  3. In a large mixing bowl, blend 2½ cups sugar, the pumpkin puree, 2 eggs, and ½ tsp baking soda. If using a mixer, use the paddle attachment.
  4. Add 2 cups flour, 2 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp cinnamon, ¼ tsp vanilla, and a pinch of salt. Blend until well mixed.
  5. Add 2½ cups milk and 4 tbsp melted butter and whisk until well mixed. If using a mixer, switch to the whisk attachment.
  6. Pour the batter into the crusts, filling them right up to the edge. The batter will be more liquid than solid, but it rises and sets in the oven.
  7. Bake at 325°F for 55 minutes.
  8. Remove from the oven and let cool.
  9. Top with 1 tsp sifted powdered sugar, then zest the orange over the top. A microplane is best, but a regular zester is fine. The best-looking version is to peel the orange and finely dice the peel, but that's a hassle.

Cardamom whipped cream

  1. Put a metal mixing bowl and whisk in the freezer for at least 15 minutes.
  2. Whisk 2 tbsp powdered sugar, 1 tsp cardamom, and a pinch of nutmeg together in the chilled bowl.
  3. Add 1 cup heavy cream and whisk until stiff peaks start to form.
  4. Serve on the cooled pie.
