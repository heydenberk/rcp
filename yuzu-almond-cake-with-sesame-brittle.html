Yuzu Almond Cake With Sesame Brittle

Yuzu Almond Cake with Sesame Brittle. Serves 8. Served with a plum wine sabayon and ultra-thin slices of kaki (Japanese persimmon). Yuzu zest and juice can be replaced with Sicilian lemon.

Ingredients

Cake

  • 300 grams caster (superfine) sugar (1 1/2 cups)
  • 300 grams unsalted butter, softened (1 1/3 cups / ~2 2/3 sticks), plus extra for greasing
  • 300 grams ground almonds (almond flour, 2 1/2 cups)
  • 4 units large eggs
  • 110 grams polenta (cornmeal) (1 cup)
  • grated zest of 2 units yuzu (or Sicilian lemons)
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • pinch of sea salt
  • 80 ml unsalted yuzu juice (1/3 cup)

Sesame brittle

  • 100 grams caster (superfine) sugar (1/2 cup)
  • 20 grams toasted white sesame seeds

Plum wine sabayon

  • 4 units large egg yolks
  • 50 grams caster sugar (1/4 cup)
  • 80 ml Japanese plum wine (1/3 cup)
  • pinch of salt
  • 120 ml double (heavy) cream (1/2 cup)
  • 2 units kaki fruit (Japanese persimmon)

You will need: a 30 cm (12 in) round cake tin and a silicone baking sheet.

Directions

  1. 10 minutesPreheat the oven to 190°C (375°F), and line and grease a 30 cm (12 in) round cake tin.
  2. 15 minutesIn a stand mixer, beat the 300g sugar and 300g softened butter until light and fluffy. Stir in the 300g ground almonds, then beat in the 4 eggs one at a time. In a separate bowl, combine the 110g polenta with the zest of 2 yuzu, 1 tsp baking powder, and a pinch of sea salt; fold into the butter mixture. Pour in the 80ml yuzu juice and fold until everything is evenly incorporated. Transfer to the tin.
  3. 35 minutesBake for 35 minutes.
  4. 15 minutesCover the cake with a piece of tin foil and bake a further 10–15 minutes, until a skewer inserted in the centre comes out clean.
  5. 30 minutesCool in the tin for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool for 20 minutes before removing the parchment. Leave the oven on for the brittle.
  6. 10 minuteswhile the cake bakesSesame brittle: Meanwhile, while the cake bakes, melt the 100g sugar in a small saucepan to a caramel, tipping the pan from side to side (don't use a spoon). When fully caramelised, pour onto a silicone sheet and leave to set.
  7. 25 minutesProcess the set caramel in a food processor to the consistency of sand. Spread evenly on the silicone sheet set on a baking sheet, and return to the oven for about 20 minutes, until the caramel dust melts again. Lift the silicone sheet so the caramel runs to the edges and spreads as thinly as possible, lay flat, sprinkle with the 20g sesame seeds, and leave to set. Shatter into large shards when cool.
  8. 15 minutes activethen 45 minutes chillingwhile the cake bakesPlum wine sabayon: Place the 4 egg yolks, 50g sugar, 80ml plum wine and a pinch of salt in a bowl set over a pan of simmering water; whisk until thickened, 8–10 minutes. Take off the heat and stir occasionally to cool. In a separate bowl, whip the 120ml cream to thick peaks; when the egg mixture is lukewarm, fold in the cream. Chill at least 45 minutes (up to 2 hours).
  9. 10 minutesTo serve, place two ultra-thin slices of kaki fruit on each plate, add a spoonful of sabayon, a slice of cake, and decorate with shards of sesame brittle.

Full example schedule

  1. 2:00 PM: Preheat oven, line and grease the cake tin
  2. 2:10 PM: Make the cake batter
  3. 2:25 PM: Cake into the oven (bake 35 min); meanwhile make the sesame caramel and start the plum wine sabayon
  4. 3:00 PM: Foil the cake, bake a further 10–15 minutes; skewer-test
  5. 3:15 PM: Cake out to cool (10 min in tin, 20 min on rack), oven left on; process caramel to sand, spread, melt ~20 min, thin out, sprinkle sesame, set and shatter
  6. 3:45 PM: Sabayon chilled and ready
  7. 4:00 PM: Slice cake; plate with kaki, sabayon, and brittle shards
