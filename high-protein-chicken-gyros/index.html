High-Protein Chicken Gyros with Yogurt Flatbread

Lean grilled chicken in a yogurt-lemon marinade, soft nonfat-yogurt flatbreads, and a lightened high-protein tzatziki. Nonfat Greek yogurt does triple duty across all three components to keep fat low while protein stays high.

Makes: 4 gyrosEstimated Total Time: ~1 hour (plus 30 min – 12 hr marinade)Approx. per gyro: 576 cal · 59g protein · 49g carbs · 14g fat

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 1.5 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 1/2 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • juice of 1 lemon
  • 3 units garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp salt

Yogurt Flatbread

  • 1.5 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil (for dough and pan)

Lightened Tzatziki

  • 1 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 English cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
  • 2 units garlic cloves, minced
  • juice of 1/2 lemon
  • 2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • pinch of salt

Veggies

  • 2 units tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 cups romaine lettuce, shredded

Directions

  1. 10 minutes activethen 30 min marinatingMarinate the chicken. Whisk the 1/2 cup yogurt, 1 tbsp olive oil, juice of 1 lemon, 3 cloves garlic, 1 tbsp oregano, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp coriander, and 1 tsp salt. Add the chicken (butterflied or pounded to even thickness) and coat well. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes (up to 12 hours; longer and the yogurt over-tenderizes the meat).
  2. 10 minuteswhile the chicken marinatesMake the tzatziki. Stir together the 1 cup yogurt, 1 tbsp olive oil, grated cucumber, 2 cloves garlic, juice of 1/2 lemon, 2 tbsp dill, and a pinch of salt. Refrigerate so the flavors meld while you cook.
  3. 10 minuteswhile the chicken marinatesMix the flatbread dough. Combine the 1.5 cups flour, 1 tsp baking powder, and 1/2 tsp salt, then stir in the 1 cup yogurt and about half the olive oil until a shaggy dough forms. Knead on a floured surface 2–3 minutes until smooth. Divide into 4 balls and roll each thin.
  4. 15 minutesCook the flatbreads. Heat a dry or lightly oiled skillet over medium-high. Cook each flatbread until puffed and charred in spots, about 1.5–2 minutes per side. Keep warm under a towel.
  5. 20 minutesGrill the chicken. Wipe off excess marinade. Grill or sauté over medium-high heat 4–6 minutes per side until the internal temp hits 165°F. Rest 5 minutes, then slice thin.
  6. 5 minutesAssemble. Lay sliced chicken on each warm flatbread. Top with tomatoes, red onion, romaine, and a generous spoon of tzatziki. Fold and eat.

Full example schedule

  1. 5:00 PM: Whisk the marinade, coat the chicken, refrigerate (30 min minimum)
  2. 5:10 PM: Make the tzatziki and refrigerate
  3. 5:20 PM: Mix and knead the flatbread dough; divide into 4 and roll thin
  4. 5:30 PM: Cook the flatbreads; keep warm under a towel
  5. 5:45 PM: Grill the chicken (4–6 min/side), rest 5 min, slice
  6. 6:05 PM: Assemble and eat

Notes & Tweaks

  • Yogurt choice matters most. Use a 0% Greek yogurt with ~23-25g protein per cup (Fage Total 0%, Chobani Non-Fat). "Greek-style" yogurts are often lower in protein — check the label if tracking tightly.
  • More protein, fewer carbs: replace 2-3 tbsp of the flour with vital wheat gluten.
  • The olive oil is intentional. Kept to 1 tbsp per component — it carries flavor and helps absorb the veggies' fat-soluble nutrients without spiking total fat.
  • Marinade macros vary with how much clings vs. drips off during cooking; scraping the chicken clean drops the fat slightly.
