9x13 Pan Pizza

Main

A same-day olive-oil dough pressed into two oiled 9x13 pans. A short par-bake sets the crust before toppings go on, which keeps the bottom crisp and not soggy. Makes two 9x13 pizzas. About 3 hours from mixing to slicing.

Ingredients

  • 780 grams all-purpose flour (6 cups)
  • 600 grams warm water, 105–110°F (2½ cups)
  • 20 grams Diamond Crystal kosher salt (2 tbsp)
  • 54 grams olive oil, for the dough (4 tbsp)
  • 8 grams sugar (2 tsp)
  • 7 grams instant yeast (2¼ tsp)
  • 30–45 grams olive oil, for the pans (2–3 tbsp)
  • Sauce, cheese, and toppings

Directions

  1. 5 minutesIn a large bowl, combine 780g flour, 20g Diamond Crystal kosher salt, 8g sugar, and 7g instant yeast. Whisk thoroughly so the salt and yeast are evenly distributed.
  2. 5 minutesAdd 600g warm (~107°F) water and 54g olive oil. Stir until a shaggy, cohesive dough forms.
  3. 10 minutesKnead by hand or in a mixer 8–10 minutes, until smooth, elastic, and slightly tacky. Avoid adding extra flour unless the dough is truly sticky.
  4. 90 minutesForm the dough into a ball, lightly oil the bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm spot ~90 minutes, until roughly doubled in size.
  5. 20 minutesTurn the dough out and divide into 2 equal pieces (~735g each). Shape gently into balls, lightly oil, cover, and rest 20 minutes to relax the gluten. Preheat the oven to 450°F now.
  6. 10 minutesGenerously oil two 9x13 pans with 15–20g olive oil per pan. Place one dough ball in each pan, flip to coat, and gently press outward. If the dough resists, pause briefly, then keep going until it reaches the corners.
  7. 5-7 minutesPar-bake the empty crusts at 450°F for 5–7 minutes, until just set and lightly puffed, with no browning.
  8. 5 minutesAdd sauce, cheese, and toppings. Keep sauce and wet toppings light to avoid sogginess.
  9. 10-15 minutesReturn the pizzas to the oven for 10–15 minutes, until the cheese is bubbling and lightly browned and the bottoms are golden and crisp.
  10. 5 minutesLet the pizzas set briefly so the structure firms up, then slice and serve.

Full example schedule

  1. 3:00 PM: Mix dry ingredients
  2. 3:05 PM: Add water and oil
  3. 3:10 PM: Knead (8–10 min)
  4. 3:20 PM: Bulk rise (~90 min)
  5. 4:50 PM: Divide and rest (20 min); preheat oven to 450°F
  6. 5:10 PM: Pan and stretch
  7. 5:20 PM: Par-bake crusts (5–7 min)
  8. 5:30 PM: Top the pizzas
  9. 5:35 PM: Final bake (10–15 min)
