Toasted Bincho Dust Marshmallows

Toasted Bincho Dust Marshmallows. Makes 18 (plenty for 10 people). The activated charcoal gives a binchotan theme; see variations for yuzu and matcha versions. Start a day in advance.

Ingredients

  • 7 sheets gelatine
  • 275 grams caster (superfine) sugar (1 1/3 cups)
  • 140 grams golden syrup (light corn syrup)
  • 80 ml water (1/3 cup)
  • 10 capsules activated charcoal (available from health-food stores)
  • 50 grams icing (powdered) sugar
  • 50 grams cornflour (cornstarch)
  • vegetable oil spray

You will need: a 20 x 30 cm (8 x 12 in) baking tray and 21 long, round wooden skewers soaked in water for 30 minutes.

Directions

  1. 5 minutesSoak the 7 sheets gelatine in plenty of cold water, and oil the baking tray with vegetable oil spray.
  2. 15 minutesMelt the 275g sugar and 140g golden syrup with 80ml water in a heavy-based saucepan. Place a jam thermometer in the pan and heat, watching closely, until the mixture reaches 122°C (250°F).
  3. 10 minutesMeanwhile, squeeze out the soaked gelatine, place it in a bowl set over a pan of simmering water, and stir gently until melted.
  4. 8 minutesPut the melted gelatine into the bowl of a stand mixer and begin whisking while you wait for the sugar to reach temperature. When the sugar is ready, decant it into a metal jug and add it to the mixer in a thin drizzle down the side of the bowl, mixer on full speed. Whisk until the mixture thickens into a sticky, smooth mass.
  5. 2 minutesWhile the machine runs, cut open the 10 charcoal capsules and pour the contents into a small bowl. Add the charcoal dust to the mixture and keep mixing until fully incorporated.
  6. 5 minutesthen 24 hoursPour into the oiled tray, smooth the top with a warm palette knife, and leave to stand 24 hours.
  7. 10 minutesThe next day, combine the 50g icing sugar with the 50g cornflour in a bowl. Lay a piece of baking parchment on a large board and turn out the marshmallow. Cut twice horizontally into three equal blocks, then six times vertically, to make 18 pieces.
  8. 15 minutesClean and dry the baking tray and add the icing-sugar/cornflour mixture. Roll 4–5 marshmallows at a time until fully coated, thread onto the soaked wooden skewers, and place on a tray. Repeat until all are done. Cover with a clean tea towel and set aside.
  9. 5 minutes per roundserve Day 2When ready for dessert, invite guests to toast their own marshmallows over the bincho coals until soft and warm. Eat immediately. The marshmallows are ready from ~12:55 PM Day 2 and hold for hours, so toast whenever dessert is served.

Variations

  • Yuzu: Add 1 1/2 tbsp yuzu juice to the sugar mixture in place of the charcoal capsules. Before pouring the mixture into the tray to set, chop 50g candied yuzu and spread it in the base of the tray.
  • Matcha: Sift 1 tbsp matcha powder into the sugar mixture in place of the charcoal capsules.
  • Fresh peach: Peel and pit 2–3 ripe peaches, purée smooth, and simmer over medium-low (stirring often) down to a thick, jammy paste — from ~1 cup purée to about 1/4 cup (3–4 tbsp) — to drive off the water and concentrate the flavor. Cool completely (a squeeze of lemon keeps the color bright). Cut the water in the syrup from 80 ml to ~60 ml to leave room for the fruit. When the mixture has whipped to a thick, sticky mass (in place of the charcoal step), drizzle in ~3 tbsp of the cooled peach reduction and whip just until incorporated — adding it at the whip stage, not the boiling syrup, avoids scorching and keeps the flavor fresh. Optionally spoon a few dollops of reduction into the base of the tray before pouring for a peach ribbon. Skip raw diced peach, which weeps and makes wet spots over the 24-hour set.

Substitutions: light corn syrup (or agave nectar / honey, 1:1) in place of golden syrup; 2 envelopes (~12 g) powdered gelatine, bloomed in a few tbsp cold water, in place of the 7 sheets.

Full example schedule

  1. 12:10 PM Day 1: Soak gelatine, oil tray
  2. 12:15 PM Day 1: Boil sugar/syrup/water to 122°C; melt gelatine over simmering water
  3. 12:30 PM Day 1: Drizzle sugar into gelatine in stand mixer, whisk to a sticky mass
  4. 12:38 PM Day 1: Mix in charcoal dust
  5. 12:40 PM Day 1: Pour into tray, smooth, stand 24 hours
  6. 12:40 PM Day 2: Combine coating, turn out and cut into 18 pieces
  7. 12:50 PM Day 2: Coat, skewer, cover until serving
  8. Day 2 dessert time: Toast over coals and serve (ready from ~12:55 PM, holds for hours)
