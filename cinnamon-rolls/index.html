Cinnamon Rolls

Ingredients

Tangzhong (Starter)

  • 30 grams bread flour (1/4 cup)
  • 180 grams water (3/4 cup)

Dough

  • 440 grams bread flour (3 2/3 cups)
  • 67 grams granulated sugar (1/3 cup)
  • 6 grams instant yeast (2 tsp)
  • 4 grams fine sea salt (3/4 tsp)
  • 80 grams heavy cream (1/3 cup, room temperature)
  • 80 grams whole milk (1/3 cup, room temperature)
  • 1 large egg (room temperature)
  • 1 tsp vanilla bean paste
  • 113 grams salted European butter (8 tbsp, cool room temperature)

Cinnamon Filling

  • 170 grams salted European butter (12 tbsp, cool room temperature)
  • 150 grams light brown sugar (3/4 cup packed)
  • 2 tbsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tsp vanilla powder (optional)

Cream Cheese Frosting

  • 85 grams unsalted butter (6 tbsp, room temperature)
  • 140 grams cream cheese (5 oz, cold)
  • 120 grams powdered sugar (1 cup)
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla bean paste
  • 1 tbsp heavy cream

Directions

  1. Line a 9x13 metal baking pan with parchment paper.
  2. 5 minutesMake tangzhong: Whisk 180g water and 30g bread flour in a saucepan. Cook over medium heat for 4-5 minutes, whisking constantly, until paste-like. Set aside to cool.
  3. 5 minutesMix dough: In stand mixer bowl, combine 440g flour, 67g sugar, 6g yeast, and 4g salt. Add 80g cream, 80g milk, 1 egg, 1 tsp vanilla, and the tangzhong. Knead on low speed for 2 minutes.
  4. 15 minutesAdd 113g butter one tablespoon at a time on low speed. Continue kneading 10-12 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  5. 30 minutesShape dough into a ball, seam-side down. Cover and rest 30 minutes.
  6. While dough rests, prepare filling: Combine 170g butter, 150g brown sugar, 2 tbsp cinnamon, and 2 tsp vanilla powder.
  7. 10 minutesRoll dough into a 15x21-inch rectangle. Spread filling across surface, leaving a 1-inch clean border at top.
  8. 10 minutesCut twelve 1 3/4-inch strips with pizza cutter. Roll each strip away from you. Transfer to baking pan.
  9. 90 minutesCover and let rise in warm place until doubled, approximately 1-2 hours. Preheat oven to 325°F/162°C during final 15 minutes.
  10. 35 minutesBake 30-35 minutes until tops are lightly golden brown (internal temp 185°F for gooey texture).
  11. While baking, make frosting: Beat 140g cream cheese and 85g butter until combined. Mix in 120g powdered sugar. Beat until light and fluffy. Add 1/2 tsp vanilla and 1 tbsp cream.
  12. 30 minutesCool pan on wire rack for 15 minutes. Spread frosting on top. Let sit another 15 minutes before serving warm.

Full example schedule

  1. 9:30 AM: Make tangzhong
  2. 9:35 AM: Mix dough ingredients
  3. 9:50 AM: Finish kneading with butter
  4. 10:20 AM: End first rise, prepare filling
  5. 10:30 AM: Roll out dough, spread filling
  6. 10:40 AM: Shape rolls, place in pan
  7. 12:10 PM: End second rise, preheat oven
  8. 12:45 PM: Remove from oven, make frosting
  9. 1:15 PM: Serve warm with frosting
