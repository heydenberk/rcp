Butternut Squash Mac and Cheese

Main

Love & Lemons' butternut squash mac and cheese. Roasted squash and shallots are blended into the base of the sauce instead of a flour-and-butter roux, then cheddar, Gruyère and pecorino are melted in. It comes out very creamy and a little sweet. The shells go into a 9x13 dish under a panko topping and get a quick broil. Serves 8.

Serves: 8Estimated Total Time: ~1 1/2 hours (about 40 minutes active)

Ingredients

Roasted Vegetables

  • 1 large butternut squash (about 3 lb; you need 2 cups / ~480g of roasted flesh)
  • 2 shallots, roughly chopped (about 2/3 cup; ~100g)
  • extra-virgin olive oil, salt and pepper, for roasting

Squash Sauce

  • 55 grams extra-virgin olive oil (1/4 cup)
  • 2 tsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1 1/2 tsp sea salt
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground sage
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg
  • freshly ground black pepper
  • 355 grams reserved pasta water (1 1/2 cups), from cooking the pasta

Pasta and Cheese

  • 454 grams shell pasta (1 lb), or pipe rigate or elbows
  • 360 grams unsweetened almond milk (1 1/2 cups), or whole or 2% milk
  • 170 grams sharp cheddar (6 oz), grated
  • 170 grams Gruyère (6 oz), grated
  • 43 grams pecorino romano or Parmesan (1 1/2 oz), finely grated

Topping

  • 40 grams panko breadcrumbs (3/4 cup)
  • 1 1/2 tsp extra-virgin olive oil

Directions

  1. 10 minutesthen roast 40–50 minutesRoast the squash and shallots. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment and oil a broiler-safe 9x13-inch baking dish (metal or ceramic; glass dishes like Pyrex aren't rated for the broiler). Halve the squash lengthwise and scoop out the seeds. Drizzle the cut sides with olive oil, season with salt and pepper and set them cut side down on the sheet. Put the 2 shallots on a square of foil, drizzle with oil, sprinkle with salt and wrap tightly. Set the packet on the sheet. Roast until the squash is very soft when poked with a knife, 40 to 50 minutes.
  2. 5 minutesthen 10 minutes to boilwhile the squash roastsMake the topping and boil the water. In a small bowl, toss the 40g panko with the 1 1/2 tsp olive oil. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
  3. 12 minutesCook the pasta. Cook the 454g shells until al dente, following the package time. The pasta only gets a quick broil later, so cook it fully now. Before draining, scoop out and reserve 355g (1 1/2 cups) pasta water. Drain.
  4. 5 minutesBlend the squash sauce. When the squash is out, switch the oven to broil and move a rack to about 6 inches below the element. Scoop out 2 cups (~480g) of the soft squash and put it in a blender with the roasted shallots, 55g olive oil, 2 tsp balsamic, 1 1/2 tsp salt, 1 tsp onion powder, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp sage, 1/4 tsp nutmeg and several grinds of pepper. Add 240g (1 cup) of the pasta water, holding back the rest. Blend until completely smooth.
  5. 10 minutesMake the cheese sauce. Put the empty pasta pot back on medium heat and warm the 360g almond milk. Stir in the squash puree and cook, stirring often, until warmed through, about 3 minutes. Turn the heat down to low. Whisking constantly, add the 170g cheddar, 170g Gruyère and 43g pecorino a handful at a time, letting each addition melt before adding the next. When the sauce is smooth, stir in the pasta. If it's thick, loosen it with splashes of the reserved pasta water.
  6. 5 minutesTop and broil. Transfer the pasta to the prepared dish and scatter the panko evenly over the top. Broil until the crumbs are crisp and golden, 2 to 5 minutes. Watch it the whole time, since broilers vary a lot and panko goes from golden to burnt in under a minute.
  7. 15 minutesRest. Let it stand 15 minutes before serving. The sauce thickens and sets up as it sits.

Full example schedule

  1. 5:00 PM: Preheat to 425°F, prep the squash and shallot packet, roast 40–50 min
  2. 5:30 PM: Toss the panko topping; put the pasta water on to boil
  3. 5:40 PM: Cook the pasta; reserve 1 1/2 cups water, drain
  4. 5:55 PM: Squash out, broiler on; blend the squash sauce with 1 cup pasta water
  5. 6:00 PM: Warm milk and puree, melt in the cheeses on low, stir in the pasta
  6. 6:10 PM: Into the dish, top with panko, broil 2–5 min
  7. 6:15 PM: Rest 15 minutes
  8. 6:30 PM: Serve

Notes & Tweaks

  • Go by the cheese weights. The original lists "about 2 1/2 cups" next to 6 oz of each cheese. Grated cheese packs very differently by volume, so use the gram weights. Grate it yourself. Pre-shredded cheese is coated in anti-caking starch and won't melt smoothly.
  • Pasta water: 1 cup first, not 1 1/2. The original blends all 1 1/2 cups into the puree. Several cooks found the result too runny, so this version starts with 1 cup and keeps the rest for adjusting. The sauce also thickens a lot during the 15-minute rest. It's meant to be loose and saucy, not a firm, sliceable baked mac.
  • Keep the heat low for the cheese. A grainy sauce usually means the cheese went in over too much heat. Use low heat and add it gradually.
  • Broil time is shorter than the original. The original says 3–10 minutes. Readers reported burning at 2–3 minutes, and the developer said they'd shorten the range. Start checking at 2 minutes.
  • Olive oil: the 1/4 cup goes into the blender. The topping gets its own 1 1/2 tsp, and roasting takes a little extra for drizzling.
  • Milk: whole or 2% cow's milk works in place of almond milk, and makes the sauce richer.
  • Color: balsamic dulls the puree toward brown. For a brighter orange, use white balsamic or apple cider vinegar.
  • Make ahead: roast the squash and shallots up to 4 days ahead (refrigerated) or freeze them for 3 months. Or finish through step 5 the day before and refrigerate the sauced pasta in the baking dish. To serve, add the panko, bake at 350°F for 20–30 minutes until hot, then broil briefly to brown the top. The baked dish freezes well too.
  • Precut squash: roast it covered in foil until soft.
  • Gluten-free: use gluten-free pasta and gluten-free panko.
  • Serve with a green salad, roasted broccoli or Brussels sprouts. It also works as a Thanksgiving side.
