Waffles

Breakfast

Melissa Clark's classic waffles, from NYT Cooking. Airy, delicate and crisp-edged, and the batter comes together in one bowl of dry and one of wet, with no resting or whipping egg whites. Yogurt gives the tang and the lift; sour cream or buttermilk work too. Makes about 10 waffles. About 35 minutes start to finish.

Makes: about 10 wafflesEstimated Total Time: ~35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 85 grams unsalted butter (6 tbsp; 3/4 stick), plus more for the waffle iron
  • 240 grams all-purpose flour (2 cups)
  • 15 grams sugar (1 tbsp)
  • 4 grams baking powder (1 tsp)
  • 5 grams fine sea salt (1 tsp)
  • 3 grams baking soda (1/2 tsp)
  • 245 grams plain yogurt (1 cup; or see note)
  • 240 grams milk (1 cup)
  • 4 large eggs

No yogurt? Sour cream or buttermilk both work 1:1. Or use another 1 cup milk mixed with 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar.

Directions

  1. 3 minutesMelt the 85g butter on the stove or in the microwave and set aside. Start preheating the waffle iron.
  2. 7 minutesIn a large bowl, whisk together the 240g flour, 15g sugar, 4g baking powder, 5g salt and 3g baking soda. In a separate bowl, whisk together the 245g yogurt, 240g milk, melted butter and 4 eggs. Fold the wet ingredients into the dry.
  3. 25 minutesLightly coat the hot waffle iron with butter, using a pastry brush or paper towel. Cook the waffles, using about 1/2 cup batter per waffle, until golden and crisp. Butter the iron between batches as needed. Serve each waffle as soon as it's ready, or hold them in a 200°F oven until everything is cooked.

Full example schedule

  1. 9:00 AM: Melt butter, preheat waffle iron
  2. 9:03 AM: Mix dry and wet ingredients, fold together
  3. 9:10 AM: Cook waffles, serving as they come off the iron (or hold in a 200°F oven)
  4. 9:35 AM: Last waffle out
